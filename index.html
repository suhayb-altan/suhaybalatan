About

I run Heyllan,a rent and building management platform — it tracks rent, tenants, and maintenance online, all in one place. Estate Link runs on it today, handling relocation and rent management for landlords and tenants in Mogadishu, from payments and receipts to maintenance requests and lease renewals. Instead of managing a building's records across scattered notes and phone calls, Heyllan keeps everything organized and accessible for both sides, from move-in to renewal.
